## Updating contact details
Open `index.html`, scroll to the `CONTACT` block near the bottom, fill in the values and push.
Any value left empty is hidden on the card. GitHub Pages republishes in about a minute.

## Sharing
- **Share** button (top-right of the photo) opens the phone's share sheet: AirDrop on iPhone, Quick Share / Nearby Share on Android, plus WhatsApp etc.
- **QR** button shows the card's QR on screen so someone can scan it from your phone, with Copy link and Download QR.
- **Write to NFC tag** appears in the QR popup only in Chrome on Android. It writes the card link to a blank NFC tag/card; any phone tapped on it opens the card.
