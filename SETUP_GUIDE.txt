4. SHARE, QR AUR NFC
   - Share button (photo ke upar right side): iPhone par AirDrop, Android par Quick Share / Nearby Share,
     WhatsApp waghera ka share menu khulta hai.
   - QR button: card ka QR screen par dikhata hai. Saamne wala seedha aapke phone se scan kar sakta hai.
     Wahin "Copy link" aur "Download QR" bhi hai.
   - qr-code.png (repository mein) print ke liye 1024px ka QR hai. Yeh seedha card ka link kholta hai aur kabhi expire nahi hota.
   - NFC: Android phone mein Chrome se card kholein -> QR -> "Write to NFC tag" -> blank NFC card/sticker phone ke
     peeche lagayein. Uske baad koi bhi phone us NFC card se touch karega to yeh visiting card khul jayega.
     (iPhone par website NFC tag write nahi kar sakti; iPhone se likhna ho to "NFC Tools" app use karein.)
